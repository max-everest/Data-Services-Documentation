# Team Members

This page lists the individuals who support the Data Services platform, their responsibilities, and their areas of expertise. Update the bios, contact preferences, and badge colors as the roster changes.

| Name | Role | Specialism | Notes |
| ---- | ---- | ---------- | ----- |
| Platform Lead | Oversees architecture and strategy | Data platform design, governance | First point for platform-wide decisions; connects with partners.
| Data Engineering | Builds and maintains pipelines | Batch/stream orchestration, data quality | Cover pipeline health and deployment coordination.
| Data Operations | Runs monitoring and incident response | Alert routing, RCA, postmortems | Maintains escalations and runbook updates.
| Integration Engineering | Owns external system Integrations | API contracts, vendor handoffs, retries | Provides liaison for partner and vendor teams.

## Maintaining the roster

- Document new hires, contractors, or assistants in this table.
- Update the "Specialism" column with the most important capability the person brings to the platform.
- Use this page to describe where someone sits in rotations or who is primary backup for a capability.
